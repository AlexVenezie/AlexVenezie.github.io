---
title: Anisotropic viscoelastic lattice phantoms for MRE
summary: 3D-printed lattice structures that reproduce the directional, viscoelastic behavior of brain white matter, giving MRE a known ground truth.
order: 1
---

Magnetic resonance elastography (MRE) estimates tissue stiffness non-invasively, but validating it in anisotropic tissue such as white matter is hard because the true properties are unknown.

This project designs 3D-printed lattice phantoms whose effective mechanical properties are known by construction. A Gaussian process surrogate of lattice homogenization lets us run inverse design: choose the target properties, then find a lattice that produces them.
